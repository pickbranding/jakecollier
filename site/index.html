The idea

Stuck at $1M is rarely a traffic problem.

Most founders who stall think they need more ads or more emails. Usually customers bought once and didn't feel anything worth coming back for. The second sale is earned by meaning, not markdowns.

i.

Watch the second order

Second-order rate, time to second order and 90-day LTV tell you more about your growth ceiling than your ROAS ever will.

ii.

Meaning over markdowns

Discounts rent loyalty. A brand people understand earns it, in the welcome email, the packaging and every touch after the first order.

iii.

Remember why you started

Founders lose the plot as they scale. Reconnecting to why the brand exists fixes the marketing faster than any new channel.

About

Performing Musician Performance Marketer Creative Director

I started out leading rooms full of people through music, first as a guitar player and then as a worship pastor for several years. The job was always the same: take people somewhere that means something, and use art as the pathway to that meaning.

Then I co-founded Weldmonger and found out that brands work the same way. We grew it from $100K to $4M a year, and most of that growth came from customers who understood what we stood for and kept coming back.

I've spent the years since running brand and retention for DTC companies, from a mushroom chocolate launch to acting creative director through a full rebrand. I learned this craft in the inbox. I stay for the bigger question underneath it: why should anyone care about what you've built?

That's the work. Help founders remember why they started, then build a brand that makes their customers feel it too.
